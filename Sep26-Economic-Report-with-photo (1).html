2026

Summary

  • During the last week of September, the average gasoline price in the Midwest was $4.2911, which was about $1.363 higher than at this time last year. This is due to the uncertainty over the ongoing conflicts in Iran.
  • In August, national energy inflation increased by 16.05% year-over-year. Energy inflation in the Midwest was slightly higher at 18.09%2. Energy inflation is the highest since October 2022 (COVID-era) for both. It fell over the past two months but has now started rising again, likely due to the ongoing conflict with Iran.
  • National Headline CPI increased by 3.40% year over year in August 2026. This was an increase from 3.36% in July. Following the national trend, the Midwestern Headline CPI in August 2026 increased 4.08% year over year and increased by 0.28 percentage points month over month.
  • Core CPI, both at the national and local levels, is increasing year-over-year by 2.45% and 3.09%, respectively, in August, while in July it increased by 2.48% and 2.90% at the national and local levels.
  • In August 2026, the national unemployment rate remained low at 4.14%, which was 4.09% in July.
  • In July 2026, Missouri’s unemployment rate ticked up to 3.90%. The unemployment rate in Columbia, MO increased by about 0.32 percentage points during this period, from 3.64% in June to 3.96% in July 2026. Although Columbia’s unemployment rate was higher than Missouri’s, the July 2026 rate was lower than in July 2025 and 2024.
  • In August 2026, the median home price in Boone County remained flat when compared to the same month last year. However, the median home price in August fell 7.27% from the previous month.
  • The median home price in Boone County in August 2026 was $350,000, which was around $50,100 higher than the median price in Missouri.
  • Boone County home sales in August 2026 totaled 173; roughly 27 fewer homes were sold than in August 2025. This marks the largest year-over-year monthly decline since June 2023.
  • Sales tax collections in September 2026 (July 2026 tax period) totaled around $5.35 million, while use tax collections came to $573,838, respectively, 11.92% (or $569,728) higher and 31.86% (or -$268,341) lower than those of the same month last year. Use tax collections will likely be made up next month due to a timing issue.
  • The total Sales and Use Tax collections for FY 2026 are currently 3.41% (or $2.09 million) higher than the same period for FY 2025.
  • Marijuana-related excise tax collection in September 2026 (July 2026 tax period) was $69,367, up by 6.36% (or $4,150 specifically) compared to September 2025. Marijuana-related excise tax collections have surpassed both FY2024 and FY2025 collections.

Glossary

Glossary of economic terms used in this report
TermDefinition
Employment
  • Worked at least 1 hour as a paid employee.
  • Worked at least 1 hour in their own business, profession, trade or farm.
  • Were temporarily absent from their job, business or farm, whether or not they were paid for the time off.
  • Worked without pay for a minimum of 15 hours in a business or farm owned by a member of their family.
Unemployment
  • They were not employed during the survey reference week.
  • They were available for work during the survey reference week, except for temporary illness.
  • They made at least one specific, active effort to find a job during the 4-week period ending with the survey reference week OR they were temporarily laid off and expecting to be recalled to their job.
Consumer Price Index (CPI)
  • A measure of the average change over time in the prices paid by consumers for a representative basket of consumer goods and services.
  • The CPI measures inflation as experienced by consumers in their day-to-day living expenses.
Inflation Rate

In this report, year-over-year changes in CPI are defined as inflation. The annual inflation rate is calculated every month as:

Inflation =CPI of latest month this year – CPI of same month last yearCPI of same month last year× 100
