
# City of Columbia - Monthly Economic Report

Interactive HTML versions of the City of Columbia Monthly Economic Report,
prepared by the Finance Department (Economic Division).

Each report is a single self-contained page with interactive charts
(hover for values), the city logo, a glossary, and a **Save as PDF** button.

## View the reports
- [September 2026](https://gularabbas.github.io/columbia-economic-reports/Sep26-Economic-Report-with-photo.html)

## Notes

- Click any link above to open the full interactive report in your browser.
- To save a copy, open a report and click **Save as PDF** (or Ctrl/Cmd + P).
- Source data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Columbia Board of Realtors,
  and the City of Columbia.
